Getting here

Travel & Parking

Driving

Church: there is plenty of parking beside the church, with disabled spaces and step-free access into the building. The church is down a country lane, just off the A272 after taking the Bolney exit from the A23.

Reception: from Bolney, you will head towards Wiston village – a 20–30 minute drive to Fairoaks Farm for our reception. Parking is available on site: please drive through the yard, past the barn and lawn, and there is a signposted parking field on the left. You are welcome to leave your car here overnight – please collect it by 10.30am the next morning.

By train

Church: Haywards Heath is the nearest station, six miles away, with fast trains from London Victoria, London Bridge and Brighton. It's a 10 minute taxi to the church.

Reception: there is no easy public transport from the church to the reception venue, so we would suggest booking a taxi or car sharing with other guests – please do let us know if you're stuck.

Taxis

Our reception venue is in a remote location, so please make sure you pre-book a taxi home for the end of the night – do not rely on an Uber being available!

Staying Over

Steyning and Bramber are the closest towns to the reception, Bolney and Cuckfield are closest to the church — either will work. There are plenty of hotels, B&Bs and Airbnbs available locally!

Questions

What do we wear?

Think summer garden party – suits and dresses, but no need for black tie. On a practical note, you will be walking around the lawn throughout the day, so block heels or flats are recommended.

Can we bring children?

Unfortunately we are unable to accommodate children outside of immediate family.

What about presents?

Your company is our main priority and we just want to celebrate with you on the day!
